**You're averaging roughly 110–130g protein/day vs 180g target.** That's ~30% below where you need to be. On veg-only (R-L) days, it drops to 80–100g.

### Why This Matters

At your current protein intake, you're losing weight — but a larger share of that weight is **muscle mass** rather than pure fat. At 180g protein, your body preserves muscle and burns fat preferentially. At 100g, you lose both. The result: you'll hit 70 kg but look "skinny-fat" instead of lean and defined.
