## Key Principles Across All Schedules

1. **Thyroxine is THE FIRST thing every morning.** Before lemon water, before brushing, before anything. 50 mcg + plain water. Then wait 30 min before any food or lemon water. Place the pill and a glass of water on your bedside table every night.
2. **No curd, milk, or paneer at breakfast.** Calcium interferes with thyroxine absorption for 4 hours. Save all calcium-rich foods for lunch onwards.
3. **Walking pad is study time**, not separate exercise time. Always pair it with notes, audio lectures, or revision.
4. **Protein at every meal.** If eating at office canteen, choose chicken/egg dish. Skip the rice if dal is available.
5. **No screens after 9:30 PM** on office days / 9:15 PM on WFH days. Blue light disrupts sleep.
6. **Rapido booking:** Pre-book the night before for morning gym slots to avoid delay.
7. **Bedside prep ritual:** Every night before bed, place thyroxine pill + water glass on bedside table. This makes the 6 AM habit automatic.

## D. Travel Day Protocol (Outstation Trips, Exams, Interviews)

When traveling for PSU exams, interviews, or any overnight trip away from home. The protocol does NOT pause — it adapts.

### Thyroxine During Travel

- Take thyroxine **before leaving home**, at the normal 6:00 AM time.
- **Pack the thyroxine strip in your carry-on bag**, never in checked luggage.
- At the hotel: place the pill + a water bottle on the nightstand before sleeping. Same bedside ritual, different room.
- Timing does not change: 6:00 AM, plain water, 30 min gap before any food.

### Hotel/Restaurant Food Rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Breakfast: boiled eggs + toast (multigrain if available) or idli/dosa. | Safe, high-protein, low-risk options available at every hotel. |
| Breakfast: NO curd, NO milk, NO butter, NO paneer. | Calcium blocks thyroxine (taken 2 hours ago). Butter is banned always. |
| Breakfast: NO fried items from the buffet. | Poori, vada, paratha = gut bomb + calorie bomb. Walk past them. |
| Lunch (1 PM+): dal + rice + curd OR grilled chicken + roti. | Curd is fine now (7+ hours after thyroxine). Protein-first. |
| Dinner: dal + roti + salad. Light and early (by 8 PM). | Heavy hotel dinners = poor sleep = poor exam/interview performance. |
| Room service: order specific items (eggs, dal, roti). | Avoids the buffet temptation trap entirely. |

### Airport / Flight Food Rules

- **Carry food:** 3–4 boiled eggs (pre-peeled, ziplock bag) + 1 apple or banana + 1 coconut water.
- **Skip airport restaurants.** Overpriced, high-sodium, heavy portions, mostly maida-based.
- **Flight meal:** If included, eat the dal/rice and skip the bread roll / dessert / butter packet.
- **Hydration:** Buy a 1L water bottle after security check. Flights dehydrate — target 500ml during flight.

### Exercise During Travel

- **No gym is expected** on travel days. Zero guilt.
- **Walk inside airports** instead of sitting at the gate. Most airports have long corridors — use them.
- **Hotel room bodyweight circuit (optional, 15 min):** 3 rounds of: 15 bodyweight squats, 10 push-ups (knees OK), 30 sec plank, 10 reverse lunges. No equipment needed.
- **Stairs over elevator** at hotels. Small NEAT win.

### Pre-Exam / Pre-Interview Day

- **Early dinner** (by 8 PM). Light — dal + roti only.
- **No new or unfamiliar food.** Hotel biryani, local street food = gut risk before an important day.
- **No caffeine after 4 PM.** Sleep quality > one more hour of revision.
- **Prep thyroxine + water on nightstand.** Do not rely on morning brain to remember.
- **Set two alarms:** one for thyroxine (6:00 AM), one for getting ready (6:30 AM).
