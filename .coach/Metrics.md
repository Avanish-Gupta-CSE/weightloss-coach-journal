## BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor Equation)

Formula: BMR = (10 x weight) + (6.25 x height) - (5 x age) + 5

BMR = (10 x 91.45) + (6.25 x 168) - (5 x 26) + 5
    = 914.5 + 1050 - 130 + 5
    = **1,839.5 kcal/day** (standard calculation)

### Hypothyroidism Adjustment

Research shows that even on properly-dosed Levothyroxine, people with hypothyroidism have 5–10% lower energy expenditure than the standard calculation predicts. The medication restores most metabolic function but not 100%.

| Scenario                         | BMR Estimate    |
| -------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Standard (no thyroid issue)      | ~1,840 kcal     |
| Hypothyroid-adjusted (5% lower)  | ~1,748 kcal     |
| Hypothyroid-adjusted (10% lower) | ~1,656 kcal     |
| **Working estimate**             | **~1,700 kcal** |

This is not a reason to eat less — the 1,900 kcal target already accounts for this. It means weight loss may be slightly slower than the textbook rate.
