update# Meal Plan — Cook's Instructions

## Daily Target: 1,900 kcal | Protein 180g | Carbs 170g | Fat 56g

Cook is available **Monday–Saturday**. Sunday is self-managed.

## CRITICAL: Thyroxine + Food Timing

Levothyroxine 50 mcg is taken at ~6:00 AM on an empty stomach. This affects what can be eaten at breakfast:

- **Breakfast (7:45–8:00 AM, ~2 hours after pill):** NO curd, NO milk, NO paneer. Calcium blocks thyroxine absorption. Eggs, roti, oats, vegetables, dal = all fine.
- **Lunch onwards (12:00 PM+, 6+ hours after pill):** Curd, milk, paneer = completely fine. No restrictions.
- **Pre-bed milk (10:00 PM):** Fine. 16 hours after pill.

**Rule: All calcium-rich foods (curd, milk, paneer) are moved to lunch, snack, or dinner. Never at breakfast.**

## Rules for the Cook

1. **Oil:** Maximum 1 teaspoon (5 ml) per dish. Use refined sunflower oil or olive oil. Measure with a teaspoon, do not pour from the bottle.
2. **Butter/Ghee/Cream:** Zero. Never. Not even "a little bit."
3. **Cooking methods:** Grilled, boiled, steamed, dry-roasted, sauteed in non-stick pan. No deep frying.
4. **Flour:** Only multigrain atta or whole wheat atta. No maida (refined flour). No white bread.
5. **Spices:** Turmeric (haldi), jeera (cumin), black pepper, coriander powder, salt. Minimal red chili — stomach is sensitive.
6. **Sugar:** Zero added sugar in any dish. No jaggery in dal. No sweetened curd.
7. **Protein in every meal:** Every single meal must have chicken, eggs, paneer, dal, or curd.
8. **Use the food scale.** Weigh all key ingredients before cooking. No eyeballing. Key weights to nail every time: 200g chicken breast (raw), 150g paneer, 60g oats, 100g cooked rice, 50g dry dal, 30g atta per roti.

## Vegetarian Breakfast Alternatives (Egg-Free, Thyroxine-Safe)

**Use these during:** Ram Navami period, veg days, or whenever besan chilla starts feeling repetitive.
**Constraints:** No paneer / curd / milk / eggs at breakfast. Protein must come from soya, dal, besan, sattu, or oats.

---

### Alt B1 — Soya Poha *(10 min — fastest option)*
- 80g thick poha (rinse under water, soak 2 min, drain)
- 60g dry soya granules — soak in hot water 5 min, squeeze all water out
- 1 tsp oil in non-stick pan → mustard seeds → curry leaves → 30g onion (sliced) → 50g tomato
- Add soya granules, mix 2 min. Add poha, haldi, salt, 1/2 tsp lemon juice at end.
- **~460 kcal / ~38g protein** | Gut-gentle, filling, completely different from chilla

---

### Alt B2 — Savory Masala Oats + Soya *(15 min)*
- 60g rolled oats + 200ml water, cook on low flame 5 min
- In parallel: 50g dry soya granules soaked in hot water 5 min, drain, squeeze
- 1 tsp oil → jeera → 30g onion → 50g tomato → haldi, salt, black pepper
- Add soaked soya, stir 2 min. Add cooked oats, mix, serve
- **~445 kcal / ~37g protein** | High fibre, very gut-friendly, great pre-gym option

---

### Alt B3 — Moong Dal Chilla *(needs overnight soak — plan night before)*
- 90g dry moong dal soaked overnight → blend to smooth batter with salt, haldi, 1 green chili
- Make 3 thin chillas on non-stick tawa, 1 tsp oil total
- Serve with 150g soya bhurji on the side (50g dry soya, onion, tomato, spices)
- **~480 kcal / ~44g protein** | Crispier and nuttier than besan chilla — feels like a different dish

---

### Alt B4 — Sattu Paratha *(if sattu is stocked)*
- 2 multigrain atta paratha dough balls (30g atta each)
- Filling: 60g sattu + 30g finely chopped onion + ajwain (1/4 tsp) + salt + lemon squeeze + water to bind
- Stuff, roll, cook on dry tawa (no oil or max 1/4 tsp per paratha)
- **~490 kcal / ~34g protein** | Very filling, good for office days with late lunches

---

### Alt B5 — Besan Chilla Upgrade (if you want to keep chilla but reduce boredom)
Rotate the fillings instead of the base:
- Version 1: Standard (onion + tomato) — the usual
- Version 2: **Spinach-corn** — add 30g corn + handful chopped palak to batter
- Version 3: **Mushroom filling** — 50g finely chopped mushroom cooked, stuffed into chilla like a wrap
- Version 4: **Soya-stuffed** — thin chilla folded around 100g soya bhurji inside

## 7-Day Rotating Menu

### Day 1 (Monday)

**Breakfast — Egg Omelette + Roti**
- 4 egg whites + 1 whole egg omelette
- Filling: 30g onion (chopped), 30g tomato (chopped), handful of spinach
- Cook in 1 tsp oil on non-stick pan
- 2 multigrain roti (medium, ~30g atta each)
- Side: 100g cucumber slices

**Lunch — Grilled Chicken + Rice + Sabzi**
- 200g raw chicken breast, cut into strips
- Marinate: 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt, black pepper, 1 tsp lemon juice
- Cook on non-stick pan with 1 tsp oil until golden (6–7 min each side)
- 100g cooked brown rice (weigh after cooking) OR 2 multigrain roti
- 150g lauki sabzi: peel and dice lauki (bottle gourd), cook with 1 tsp oil, jeera, turmeric, salt, 50ml water. Simmer until soft.
- 100g plain curd on the side

**Dinner — Chicken Curry + Roti + Salad**
- 200g raw chicken breast, cut into pieces
- Curry: 1 tsp oil, 50g onion (sliced), 100g tomato (pureed), 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp jeera, salt, black pepper
- Cook onion until soft (3 min), add tomato, cook 5 min, add chicken, 100ml water, cover and cook 15 min
- No cream, no butter, no coconut milk
- 1 multigrain roti
- Salad: 100g cucumber + 50g tomato + 30g onion + lemon juice + salt

---

### Day 2 (Tuesday)

**Breakfast — Egg Bhurji + Roti**
- 4 egg whites + 1 whole egg, scrambled
- Add: 30g onion, 1 green chili (deseeded for mild heat), 20g capsicum, turmeric, salt
- 1 tsp oil in non-stick pan
- 2 multigrain roti

**Lunch — Paneer Bhurji + Roti + Dal**
- 150g paneer, crumbled
- 1 tsp oil, 30g onion, 50g tomato, 1/2 tsp turmeric, jeera, salt
- Cook onion and tomato 3 min, add paneer, mix, cook 3 more min
- 2 multigrain roti
- 1 bowl moong dal (50g dry moong dal, boiled with turmeric, salt, jeera. No ghee tadka.)

**Dinner — Moong Dal + Rice + Sabzi**
- Large bowl moong dal (50g dry, boiled with turmeric, salt, jeera, 1/2 tsp lemon juice at the end)
- 100g cooked brown rice
- 150g tori sabzi (ridge gourd): peel, slice, cook with 1 tsp oil, jeera, turmeric, salt
- 100g curd

---

### Day 3 (Wednesday)

**Breakfast — Oats + Boiled Eggs**
- 60g rolled oats cooked in 200ml water (NOT milk — thyroxine rule), pinch of salt, 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- Top with 5 sliced almonds (unfried)
- 2 boiled eggs on the side (eat both yolks today — it's a rest day, need the fats)

**Lunch — Chicken + Roti + Palak Sabzi**
- 200g grilled chicken breast (same prep as Day 1)
- 2 multigrain roti
- 150g palak sabzi: wash and chop palak (spinach), 1 tsp oil, jeera, garlic (2 cloves minced), turmeric, salt. Cook 5 min.
- 100g curd

**Dinner — Egg Curry + Roti**
- 4 boiled eggs, halved
- Curry: 1 tsp oil, 50g onion, 100g tomato puree, 1/2 tsp turmeric, jeera, salt, black pepper
- Cook onion 3 min, tomato 5 min, add eggs, simmer 5 min
- 1 multigrain roti
- Salad: cucumber + tomato + lemon

---

### Day 4 (Thursday)

**Breakfast — Besan Chilla + Boiled Eggs**
- 40g besan (gram flour) + 60ml water + salt + turmeric + 30g onion (finely chopped) + 20g tomato (diced)
- Make 2 thin chillas on non-stick pan with 1 tsp oil total
- 2 boiled eggs on the side (NO curd at breakfast — calcium interferes with thyroxine, move curd to lunch)

**Lunch — Chicken + Rice + Beans Sabzi**
- 200g grilled chicken breast
- 100g cooked brown rice
- 150g French beans sabzi: trim and chop beans, 1 tsp oil, jeera, turmeric, salt, 50ml water, cook 8 min
- 150g plain curd on the side (moved here from breakfast — safe, 6+ hours after thyroxine)

**Dinner — Dal + Roti + Salad**
- Large bowl masoor dal (50g dry red lentils, boiled with turmeric, salt, jeera, tomato)
- 2 multigrain roti
- Salad: cucumber + onion + lemon
- 100g curd

---

### Day 5 (Friday)

**Breakfast — Egg Omelette + Oats**
- 4 egg whites + 1 whole egg omelette with onion, tomato (same as Day 1)
- 40g oats cooked in 150ml water with salt

**Lunch — Paneer Tikka (dry) + Roti + Sabzi**
- 150g paneer, cut into cubes
- Marinate: 50g thick curd, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp red chili (mild), salt, 1 tsp lemon juice. Marinate 10 min.
- Cook on non-stick pan with 1 tsp oil, turning until charred on all sides (8–10 min)
- 2 multigrain roti
- 150g bhindi sabzi: wash, dry completely, slice, cook with 1 tsp oil, turmeric, salt on medium heat (do not cover — keeps it crispy, not slimy)

**Dinner — Chicken Soup-Style Curry + Roti**
- 200g chicken breast, cut into small pieces
- 1 tsp oil, 50g onion, 2 cloves garlic (minced), 50g tomato, turmeric, jeera, black pepper, salt
- Add 300ml water. Bring to boil, simmer 15 min. (Soupy curry — easy on the gut, warming.)
- 1 multigrain roti
- Salad

### Day 6 (Saturday)

**Breakfast — Boiled Eggs + Toast**
- 3 boiled eggs (eat all yolks — Saturday is full body + HIIT day, need energy)
- 2 slices multigrain bread (NOT white bread / maida bread), toasted
- 100g cucumber
- NO milk or curd at breakfast (thyroxine rule)

**Lunch — Chicken Fried Rice (healthy version)**
- 200g chicken breast, diced small
- 100g cooked brown rice (day-old rice works best)
- 50g mixed vegetables: peas, carrot (diced), beans (chopped)
- 1 tsp oil, 2 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tsp soy sauce (low sodium), salt, black pepper
- Cook chicken first (5 min), set aside. Stir-fry veggies 3 min, add rice, soy sauce, chicken, toss 2 min.

**Dinner — Moong Dal Khichdi + Curd**
- 40g moong dal + 60g rice, washed and soaked 15 min
- Pressure cook with 300ml water, turmeric, salt, jeera (3 whistles)
- Light, easy to digest, perfect after a hard Saturday workout
- 150g curd on the side
- Salad

### Day 7 (Sunday — No Cook, Self-Managed)

**Vegetarian versions (Ram Navami / active veg period) — use these when no eggs:**

**Breakfast — Besan Chilla (tawa, not airfryer — batter dish)**
- 80g besan + water to thin batter + 30g onion (chopped) + 30g tomato + salt + pinch cumin
- Cook on non-stick pan, 1 tsp oil total, 3-4 min each side
- NO curd/milk (thyroxine rule — breakfast only)
- ~340 kcal, ~17g protein

**Lunch — Airfryer Paneer Tikka + Dal + Rice**
- 150g paneer, cubed 2 inches
- Marinade: 3 tbsp curd + 1/2 tsp turmeric + 1/2 tsp cumin powder + 1/4 tsp chili powder + pinch garam masala + salt + 1 tsp lemon + 1/2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- Marinate 15-20 min. Single layer in airfryer tray. Spritz 0.5 tsp oil.
- Air fry **180°C for 14 min**, flip at 7 min. Last 2 min at 200°C.
- + 60g dry moong dal (pressure cook: 2 cups water + turmeric + salt + jeera, 3 whistles)
- + 100g cooked rice
- ~710 kcal, ~43g protein

**Snack (~5 PM) — Airfryer Soya Chunks**
- 50g dry soya chunks — soak in hot water 20 min, drain, squeeze hard (all water out = crispy)
- Marinade: 1 tbsp curd + 1/4 tsp turmeric + 1/4 tsp cumin + pinch chili + salt + 1 tbsp besan (for crispy coat) + pinch amchur
- Single layer in airfryer. Spritz 0.5 tsp oil.
- Air fry **190°C for 14 min**, shake at 7 min.
- + 100g curd on side
- ~265 kcal, ~31g protein

**Dinner — Moong Dal Khichdi + Airfryer Paneer Bites**
- 40g moong dal + 60g rice (pressure cook together, turmeric + salt + jeera, 3 whistles)
- 80g paneer cubed, rub: salt + turmeric + jeera + black pepper. Spritz 0.5 tsp oil.
- Air fry **180°C for 10-12 min**, flip at 6 min.
- ~520 kcal, ~24g protein

**Pre-bed — Warm Milk**
- 200ml warm milk + 1/4 tsp turmeric + pinch black pepper
- ~100 kcal, ~7g protein

**Sunday Veg Day Totals (with airfryer): ~1,935 kcal | ~122g protein**
*(Up from ~1,775 kcal / ~100g on non-airfryer Sunday — airfryer adds ~22g protein via soya chunks snack)*

## Ram Navami Vegetarian Period (March 21–29, 2026) — TEMPORARY

**Duration:** 9 days. After March 29, revert to the standard 7-day rotating menu above.

**Rules:**
- Strictly vegetarian. No eggs, no chicken, no fish, no non-veg of any kind.
- All other protocol rules remain active (1 tsp oil, no maida, no deep frying, no cream/butter/ghee, no sugar-loaded sweets).
- Thyroxine rule unchanged: NO curd/milk/paneer at breakfast (4-hour calcium gap after 6:00 AM pill).
- Realistic protein target: **110–150g/day.** Hitting 180g on pure veg without eggs is near-impossible. Prioritize soya (granules and chunks), paneer, dal, besan, and curd at every meal.
