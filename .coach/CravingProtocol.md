# Craving Protocol — The 9:30 PM Battle Plan

## Why This Exists

You are most vulnerable to binge eating between 9:00 PM and midnight. The pattern is predictable:
1. You get home after a 9-hour shift (or a long study session).
2. You feel mentally drained, emotionally flat, and "deserve a reward."
3. You open Swiggy/Zomato. You order Biscoff, Kunafa, Biryani, Domino's, or heavy parottas.
4. The dopamine spike lasts 20 minutes. The guilt lasts until morning. The fat stays for weeks.

This protocol breaks that loop.

---

## Step 1: The 5-Minute Rule (MANDATORY)

When the urge hits:
- Set a timer on your phone for **5 minutes**.
- During these 5 minutes, you are **not allowed** to open any food delivery app.
- Just survive 5 minutes. That's it.

Most cravings peak and fade within 3–4 minutes. The timer outlasts the spike.

---

## Step 2: Drink (IMMEDIATELY)

Within those 5 minutes:
- Pour **500ml cold water**.
- Squeeze **half a lemon** into it.
- Drink the entire glass.

Dehydration mimics hunger. Lemon water resets the gut and gives your mouth something to taste. This kills 40% of cravings on the spot.

---

## Step 3: Walk It Off (THE WALKING PAD WEAPON)

- Get on the walking pad. Set it to **5–6 kmph**.
- Walk for **10 minutes**.
- Put on a **podcast, exam audio lecture, or music**. Not food content. Not YouTube food vlogs.

Physical movement redirects the dopamine-seeking brain. You are now burning calories instead of consuming them. After 10 minutes of walking, the craving has lost its emotional power.

---

## Step 4: The Substitute Meal

If the craving persists after Steps 1–3, eat one of these. They are pre-approved, within your calorie budget, and will physically fill your stomach:

### Sweet Craving Substitute
- **200g plain curd + 1 tsp honey + 5 cucumber slices**
- ~120 kcal, ~8g protein
- The curd is cold and creamy (replaces ice cream craving). Honey gives sweetness without a sugar bomb. Cucumber adds crunch and volume.

### Savory/Heavy Craving Substitute
- **3 boiled egg whites + salt + black pepper + chaat masala**
- ~50 kcal, ~11g protein
- Pure protein. Kills hunger signals at the hormonal level (PYY and GLP-1 release). Zero carbs, zero fat.

### "I Want Something Warm" Substitute
- **200ml warm toned milk + 1/4 tsp turmeric + pinch black pepper**
- ~100 kcal, ~7g protein
- Anti-inflammatory. Tryptophan in milk promotes sleepiness. You'll be drowsy in 20 minutes — craving gone.

---

## Step 5: Phone Lockdown

**Uninstall Swiggy and Zomato.** Right now. Today.

- The friction of re-installing the app (2–3 minutes) is enough to kill most impulse orders.
- Re-install ONLY on Sunday for weekly grocery/ingredient ordering. Then uninstall again.
- If you cannot bring yourself to uninstall, set a daily app usage limit of 0 minutes for both apps after 7:30 PM (use Digital Wellbeing / Screen Time settings).

---

## Step 6: Log the Trigger

Open `.coach/Progress.md` and write one line:

```
Craving at [time]: Trigger was [stress/boredom/exam anxiety/loneliness/habit]. Outcome: [resisted/substituted/failed].
```

Tracking triggers reveals patterns. After 2 weeks, you will know exactly what causes your binges and can pre-empt them.

Common triggers and their real solutions:
| Trigger | Real Solution |
|---|---|
| Work stress | 10 min walking pad + deep breathing |
| Exam anxiety | Review one easy topic for 15 min to rebuild confidence |
| Boredom | Walking pad + podcast. Or call a friend. |
| Loneliness | Call someone. Not Swiggy. Food is not a friend. |
| Habit (just used to ordering) | The uninstall in Step 5 breaks the habit loop |
| Actual hunger | Eat the substitute meal from Step 4. You're underfed, not craving. |

---

## Step 7: Nuclear Option

If Steps 1–6 fail and you're about to place the order:

1. Close the app.
2. Go to the kitchen.
3. Boil 4 eggs. Right now. It takes 12 minutes.
4. While they boil, drink another 500ml water.
5. Eat the 4 boiled eggs (remove 2 yolks) with salt and pepper.
6. You now have ~170 kcal of pure protein in your stomach. The hunger hormones are suppressed. The craving is biochemically impossible to sustain.

---

## Step 8: Sleep Weapon

If it's past 10:00 PM and the craving is still alive:

- Make haldi doodh (warm milk + turmeric).
- Drink it in bed.
- Set your alarm for 6:00 AM.
- Close your eyes.

**Cravings cannot win if you are asleep.** Every minute you stay awake past 10:30 PM on a craving night is a minute where your willpower is at its lowest. Sleep is the ultimate defense.

---

## Pre-Emptive Measures (Do These BEFORE the Craving Hits)

### Evening Routine Armor
1. **Eat dinner within 30 minutes of getting home.** Do not change clothes, scroll phone, or sit down first. Eat immediately. A full stomach does not crave.
2. **Get on the walking pad within 15 minutes after dinner.** This fills the "post-dinner scroll" window where most orders happen.
3. **Keep your craving kit stocked:** boiled eggs, curd, cucumber, lemons, honey. If these are not in the fridge, you will order food instead. Stock them every Sunday.

### Mental Reframe
When the urge hits, ask yourself one question:

> "Will this order make me look better on the date I want to go on?"

The answer is always no. The 20 minutes of taste are not worth the 20 days of extra deficit you'll need to undo the damage.

### The Math of a Single Binge
| Item | Calories |
|---|---|
| Domino's medium pizza (4 slices) | ~1,100 kcal |
| Biscoff cheesecake (1 slice) | ~450 kcal |
| Kunafa (1 piece) | ~350 kcal |
| Biryani (1 plate) | ~700 kcal |
| Rasmalai (2 pieces) | ~400 kcal |
| **Typical binge order** | **~1,500–2,500 kcal** |

A single binge wipes out **2–3 days of calorie deficit**. That's 2–3 days of gym, walking pad, and discipline — erased in 20 minutes.
