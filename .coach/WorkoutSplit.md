# Workout Split — Beginner Machine-First Program

## You Do NOT Need a Personal Trainer

Machines are perfect for beginners:
- Fixed path of motion guides you — you literally cannot do the movement wrong
- Every machine has an instruction sticker showing seat adjustment and form
- Progressive overload is dead simple: move the pin one notch heavier
- Research confirms machines build muscle just as effectively as free weights for beginners
- Injury risk is far lower than free weights without coaching

Your "trainer" is free: **watch one 3-minute YouTube video before trying any new machine.**

### Recommended YouTube Channels (Free Form Coaching)

- **Jeremy Ethier** — science-based, beginner-friendly, short videos. Search "Jeremy Ethier [machine name]"
- **Jeff Nippard** — detailed technique breakdowns for every exercise
- **KevTheTrainer** — full machine-only workout walkthroughs for beginners

Watch the relevant video the night before or on your Rapido ride to the gym. That's all the coaching you need.

---

## Overview

- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4):** Machine-only. Learn every machine. Build the habit.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 5–8):** Machines + basic free weights (goblet squat, DB row, DB press).
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 9+):** Full hybrid — machines, dumbbells, cables, bodyweight.
- **Walking pad:** 30–60 min daily LISS at 4–5 kmph (separate from gym, done while studying)

## Crowded Morning Gym Strategy (Office Days)

The old plan said "use dumbbells to avoid machine queues." That doesn't work for a beginner who doesn't know dumbbell form. Instead:

- **Arrive at 6:15–6:20 AM** (Cult.fit opens at 6:00). The first wave of people finishes by 6:15.
- **Have a priority order:** If your first machine is taken, move to the next one on the list. Circle back.
- **If 3+ machines are taken:** Do the Home Bodyweight Backup (bottom of this file) + 20 min walking pad. Skip the gym that day. Better than standing around.
- **Saturday afternoon (empty gym):** This is the MOST important session. Full access, no rush, learn new machines here first.

## Phase 1: Machine-Only (Weeks 1–4)

Goal: Learn every machine. Build the gym habit. Do NOT chase heavy weights. Focus on feeling the target muscle contract.

### Sets and Reps: 2 sets x 12–15 reps per exercise
### Rest: 60 seconds between sets
### Weight: Start light. If you can do 15 reps easily, go one notch heavier next session.

## Phase 2: Machines + Basic Free Weights (Weeks 5–8)

**Buy the Kore PVC 20 kg adjustable dumbbell set at the start of week 5.**

By week 5, you know every machine and have basic gym confidence. Now add 3 simple free-weight exercises that are hard to get wrong:

### New exercises to add (replace one machine exercise per day):

| Exercise | Replaces | Why It's Easy to Learn |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet Squat (hold 1 dumbbell at chest) | One set of Leg Press | One dumbbell, natural squatting motion. Watch "Jeremy Ethier goblet squat" once. |
| DB Bent-Over Row (one arm on bench) | One set of Seated Row | One dumbbell, one arm, supported position. Very stable. |
| DB Shoulder Press (standing) | One set of Shoulder Press Machine | Two dumbbells, natural pressing motion. Start light. |

### Updated sets and reps: 3 sets x 10–12 reps
### Progressive overload begins: When you can do 12 reps on the last set with good form, move up +2.5 kg on machines, +1 kg on dumbbells.

Keep all other exercises as machines. This phase is about adding variety, not replacing machines entirely.

## Phase 3: Full Hybrid (Weeks 9+)

Graduate to a full Upper/Lower split mixing machines and free weights. By now you have:
- 8 weeks of gym habit
- Solid machine form on every exercise
- Basic dumbbell competence (goblet squat, row, press)
- Dumbbells at home for backup days

### Weekly Structure:
- **Mon:** Upper Push — Chest Press Machine + DB Shoulder Press + Cable Fly + Tricep Pushdown + Lateral Raise (DB or machine)
- **Tue:** Lower + Core — Leg Press + Goblet Squat + Leg Curl + Calf Raise + Plank + Cable Crunch
- **Wed:** REST or walking pad
- **Thu:** Upper Pull — Lat Pulldown + DB Row + Cable Face Pull + Cable Bicep Curl + Cable Hammer Curl
- **Fri:** Lower + Core — Leg Press (high foot) + DB Lunges + Leg Extension + Hip Thrust Machine + Hanging Leg Raise
- **Sat:** Full Body + HIIT
- **Sun:** Rest + walking pad

### Sets and reps: 3 sets x 8–12 reps
### Progressive overload: +2.5 kg when 12 reps on last set is easy. Back to 8 reps at new weight.

## Home Bodyweight Backup (Skip-Gym Days)

If you cannot make it to the gym (all machines taken, bad weather, Rapido unavailable, late wake-up, exam stress), do this at home. No equipment needed.

**20-min Bodyweight Circuit (3 rounds, 45 sec rest between rounds):**

| Exercise | Reps |
|---|---|
| Bodyweight Squat | 15 |
| Push-ups (knees OK if needed) | 10–12 |
| Plank | 30 sec |
| Reverse Lunges (alternating) | 10/leg |
| Mountain Climbers | 20 total |

Then: 15 min walking pad at 6 kmph.

**Total time: ~35 min.** Better than skipping entirely.

Once dumbbells arrive (Phase 2, week 5), upgrade to:

| Exercise | Reps |
|---|---|
| Goblet Squat | 12 |
| DB Floor Press | 12 |
| DB Bent-Over Row (each side) | 12 |
| DB Shoulder Press (standing) | 10 |
| DB Reverse Lunge (each leg) | 10 |

Then: 15 min walking pad at 6 kmph.

## Progressive Overload Schedule

| Phase | Weeks | Sets x Reps | Weight Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | 1–4 | 2 x 12–15 | Start light. If 15 reps is easy, go one notch heavier next session. |
| Phase 2 | 5–8 | 3 x 10–12 | When 12 reps on last set is easy, add +2.5 kg (machines) or +1 kg (dumbbells). |
| Phase 3 | 9+ | 3 x 8–12 | Same rule. When 12 reps is easy, increase weight, drop back to 8 reps. Build up again. |

**Track every workout.** Use the Notes app on your phone. Write: exercise name, weight, sets x reps. Example: "Chest Press 20kg, 2x15". This takes 30 seconds between sets and is the single most important habit for progress.
